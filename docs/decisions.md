# Decisions

One dated line per non-obvious choice and why, newest last. Link to the doc that explains it in
full. Entries marked *(backfilled)* were recorded after the fact, dated from git history.

- 2026-10-14 — Build for Chrome 106 (`build.target`) and CSS for Chrome 61 (`cssTarget`): the frame's WebView 107 rejects newer syntax, and the lower CSS target stops the minifier producing 4/8-digit hex. See [frame.md](frame.md#what-the-browser-cant-do). *(backfilled; WebView figure superseded 2026-10-16)*
- 2026-10-14 — No framework, no runtime dependencies beyond fonts: every kilobyte is parsed on a Cortex-A7 with 1 GB RAM. See [frame.md](frame.md#performance-rules). *(backfilled; CPU superseded 2026-10-16)*
- 2026-10-14 — The frame talks to one origin we control and never calls third-party APIs: Android 6's trust store fails modern HTTPS chains, and keys must stay off the device. See [frame.md](frame.md#network). *(backfilled; superseded 2026-10-16)*
- 2026-10-14 — Photos never go into git; they're resized to ≤ 1280 px on the Mac and at most two are decoded at once. See [frame.md](frame.md#performance-rules). *(backfilled)*
- 2026-10-14 — Reuse the kiosk app from wesbos/photo-frame-dashboard (with permission) instead of writing one; forked into `kiosk/` on 2026-10-16. See [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md). *(backfilled)*
- 2026-10-14 — Serve hashed assets as immutable and make the 3 a.m. reload wait for the server, so a loaded dashboard keeps working while the Mac sleeps. See [frame.md](frame.md#how-the-page-gets-onto-the-frame). *(backfilled)*
- 2026-10-14 — Bundle the dashboard into a forked kiosk app (Phase 1.5) instead of waiting for the home server, to remove the Mac from boot. See [roadmap.md](roadmap.md#phase-15--no-mac-needed). *(backfilled)*
- 2026-10-16 — Retarget from the RK3126 (Android 6) to the RK3326 board that arrived: Android 8.1, WebView 106 put in `/system`. Supersedes the WebView 107 and Cortex-A7 figures above. See [frame.md](frame.md).
- 2026-10-16 — The frame may call owner-approved services directly (WMATA, Google Calendar by secret address), since the RK3326's trust store is updated; supersedes "never calls third-party APIs". Extended 2026-10-19 to weather.gov, NASA, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art and Wikimedia Commons, all without keys. See [frame.md](frame.md#network) and AGENTS.md.
- 2026-10-19 — Public pictures are measured on the frame (sharpness at the size shown, darkness) in a Worker before they're shown, and only dark ones are kept: the main thread took 130–770 ms per picture. See [design.md](design.md).
- 2026-10-19 — The Pomodoro timer is stored as timestamps, not a countdown, so it survives reloads and power cuts; it shows no countdown until network time arrives, and drops a session that ended more than 10 minutes before the frame came back. See `dashboard/src/pomodoro-data.js`.
- 2026-10-21 — The Telugu screen bundles Noto Sans Telugu (the Telugu subset, about 36 KB) instead of relying on the frame's copy, so the Mac preview and the frame draw the same letters; the sans, not the serif, because its even strokes are closest to a pen line. Each letter is moved so its ink is centred in its box (measured from the font when the page loads), at the owner's request after trying a shared baseline: letters with a mark above or a tail below looked off-centre. See `dashboard/src/screens/telugu.js`.
- 2026-10-21 — Time tracking grows out of the Pomodoro screen as one log of entries rather than a second screen: a Pomodoro is a tracked block that counts down, one thing runs at a time, so insights never count time twice. No mode switch: a category tap tracks, "Start 45 minutes" starts a Pomodoro. A tracker with no touch for 2 hours stops at the last touch, with one tap to count the rest, since the frame can't tell if you're there. The storage key and backup file keep the Pomodoro names so `adb pull` paths still hold. See `dashboard/src/pomodoro-data.js`.
- 2026-10-21 — The Time screen's daily goal is hours of focus, not Pomodoros, so tracked focus time counts too; a full Pomodoro counts as an hour with its break (the owner's rule: 2 Pomodoros and an hour tracked make 3 of 4). While tracking, the frame stays on Time but night still comes, so a forgotten tracker can't keep the screen on.
- 2026-10-21 — Every Time metric counts focus only (the focus categories, a full Pomodoro as an hour with its break): the streak is days with an hour of focus, all time is hours of focus. Doom scrolling and breaks are nudged with a chime and an orange pulse every 10 minutes, counted from when the tracker started, and never for reminders a reload skipped.
- 2026-10-21 — Chores count as focus on the Time screen, at the owner's request: Recruiting, GEICO, Masters, Projects and Chores; doom scrolling, breaks, the bathroom and Other don't.
- 2026-10-22 — The night clock asks how the evening goes (#19): "Still working" keeps it away until the screen-off hour, "Going to sleep" turns the screen off now and after each idle spell until morning. The choice lapses at a set time rather than being cleared, so the next evening needs no reset, and it's kept in `localStorage` so a reload or power cut doesn't light the room. See `dashboard/src/night.js`.
- 2026-10-24 — Consonants 1 shows only the romanization beside its eight hardest letters (ఙ ఞ ట డ ణ త ద న), not the Telugu word and meaning as on Consonants 2 (#22): at five to a row the full word was crowded, and the owner wants the sound to say out loud. ఙ and ఞ use gaṅga (గఙ్గ, classical spelling) and pañchadāra (పఞ్చదార, sugar), which the owner preferred over vāṅmayaṁ and jñānaṁ.
- 2026-10-24 — The Time screen's last 7 days became a grid of focus categories by day (#23), replacing stacked bars, so a category's gaps read at a glance; a tapped day shows its hours against usual (the average over past days with anything tracked). "What to start with" is the `balance` category longest since it last had time, minutes in the last 3 days breaking ties, since the owner plans each day by what's been left out; GEICO (set hours) and Projects aren't balanced, and Chores (`daily: 15`) counts a day only with 15 minutes and drops out once today has them. Shading cells by amount and a bar for the goal were tried in mockups and dropped as too busy.
- 2026-10-24 — The stacked bars stay as the last 7 days' first view, with the grid a swipe away (#23), at the owner's request (bars the default): the grid answers what's been left out, the bars show each day's whole mix against the goal. The view last left is kept in `localStorage` (`glance-time-week`), a per-frame preference like the night choice, not part of the Time log.
